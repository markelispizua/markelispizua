About

I shape the emotion of every image through colour.

I'm a young colourist with several independent projects behind me, including Carrerilla (2026), directed by Antonio Izco. I also develop my own projects, which I edit and grade myself from start to finish.

I studied Audiovisual Communication at the UPV/EHU, and that background shapes how I approach every grade: colour in service of the story.

I usually work with DaVinci Resolve for grading, and with Premiere Pro and After Effects for editing and finishing. I rely on scopes, LUTs, film grain and references to build images with character that support the story without overpowering it.
